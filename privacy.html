Welcome to The AI Depot. We are committed to protecting your privacy. 2. Collection of Your Information

We may collect personal information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect on the Site and through our services includes:

  • Personal Data: Personally identifiable information, such as your name, business name, email address, and telephone number, that you voluntarily give to us when you register for our services or when you choose to participate in various activities related to the Site and our services, such as consultations and support requests.

Having accurate information about you permits us to provide you with a smooth, efficient, and customized experience. Specifically, we may use information collected about you to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Provide the services you request, including the setup and management of AI assistants.
  • Email you regarding your account or order.
  • Fulfill and manage purchases, orders, payments, and other transactions related to our services.

4. Disclosure of Your Information

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third parties for their promotional purposes. We may share information we have collected about you in certain situations:

If you become aware of any data we have collected from children under age 13, please contact us immediately using the information provided below, and we will take immediate operational steps to delete the records from our servers.
